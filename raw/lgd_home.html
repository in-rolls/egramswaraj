About Local Government Directory

Local Government Directory (LGD) - https://lgdirectory.gov.in is one of the 11 applications developed as part of Panchayat Enterprise Suite (PES) under e-Panchayat Mission Mode project (MMP). The primary objective of the Local Government Directory (LGD) is to build a Standard location directory by providing an online platform to the States/UTs to maintain the up-to-date list of respective administrative units (Districts, Sub-Districts, Villages, Blocks, Local Governance bodies along with their corresponding Rural/ Urban wards) in collaboration with Office of the Registrar General of India (ORGI), Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA).

The adoption of LGD codes as the Standard location code in the e-Governance applications of Ministries/Departments/States/UTs was mandated by Cabinet Secretariat vide letter dated 04th November 2016. The agenda behind this directive from Cabinet Secretariat was to establish seamless exchange of data across all e-Governance applications and thus ensure transparency in the system.

Useful for:

  • All Central Government Ministries and Department/State Governments
  • State Panchayati Raj Departments
  • State Urban Development Departments
  • State Revenue Departments
  • State Election Commissions
  • A unique frame for all types of Census and Surveys
  • Registered Software Applications
  • Citizen

Overview

  • A collaborative work initiative between Ministry of Panchayati Raj (MoPR) and Office of the Registrar General of India (ORGI), Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) for creating unified, error free and up-to-date Jurisdictional directory in the country which could be used by all the stakeholders.
  • ORGI playing a lead role in synchronization of LGD codes and Census directory for finalization of the Uniform Jurisdictional Directory by coordinating with State Revenue Department and Urban Local Bodies.
  • Census Data available as per Uniform Jurisdictional Directory will help in informed decision-making, Equitable resource allocation, and evidence-based policy formulation across various sectors.
  • Key Features of Local Government Directory: Generation of unique code for each local government body at every level (District, Block, Village). A unique number is assigned to each local government body.
  • Maintenance of local government bodies and its mapping with constituting land region entities. For example: Mapping of Gram panchayat with villages.
  • Maintenance of Parliamentary and Assembly constituencies and their mapping to the respective covered areas as per the delimitation notice.
  • Mandatory upload of Govt. order for each modification in the directory: This checkpoint has been mandated to ascertain the LGD consumers that the data published in LGD is authentic.
  • Maintenance of historical data: When modifications are done to the existing LGD codes, the old value/data gets archived.
  • Provision to maintain state specific local government setup.
  • Facility to integrate with state specific location codes: If any state is following standard codes for state level software applications, the same code can be linked to LGD code.
